# Aster Labs — Document Research Pilot

This is a fictional sample document supplied with EvidenceLens.

## Timeline
The pilot runs for six weeks. Week 1 is for collecting approved documents and
agreeing on evaluation questions. Weeks 2 and 3 cover ingestion and retrieval.
Weeks 4 and 5 cover answer generation, citation checks, and user feedback.
Week 6 is for a final evaluation and a decision about a wider rollout.

## Success criteria
The pilot must retrieve a supporting passage in the top five results for at least
85 percent of the approved evaluation questions. Every generated factual answer
must include source references. Questions that cannot be answered from the
documents must receive a clear insufficient-evidence response.
